# Portfolio language

The public portfolio describes Waseem's practice through work, notes and a connected capability map.

## Language

**Case study**: An anonymised account of work, organised around a problem, approach and outcome. _Avoid_: client profile.

**Note**: A dated article about the practice of building and operating useful systems. _Avoid_: case study for an article that describes an idea rather than a delivered piece of work.

**Draft**: An unfinished note excluded from publication and discovery.

**Capability**: One strand of the practice, such as AI automation or learning design.

**Capability map**: The connected diagram whose selectable nodes explain those strands.

**Trace**: A connection between a capability node and the central practice in that map.

**Console**: The dark presentation surface used for orientation, diagrams and selected work.

**Essay**: The light reading surface used for longer narrative content.

**Datasheet**: The compact summary of a case study's context and outcome beside its narrative.
