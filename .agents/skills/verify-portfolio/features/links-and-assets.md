# Contact and linked assets

Open Contact and inspect its email link. Leave it unactivated; opening a real mail
application or sending a message is outside verification. External links likewise
remain unvisited. The rendered checker verifies local href/src targets.

Read the feed, sitemap and all three intentional audio HTTP URLs. Require successful
nonempty responses; the audio bodies are not played or decoded. Preserve them even
though they are unlinked from the site: `audio/README.md` explains their external
consumer. Verification neither operates nor reconfigures that consumer.
